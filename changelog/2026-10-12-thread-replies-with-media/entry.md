---
title: Add images and videos to each reply in a thread
date: 2026-10-12
tag: new
---

Threads on X, Bluesky and Mastodon can now carry media in every reply, not just the first post.

Each post in the thread shows its own media right under it. Drop, paste or upload an image or video while a reply is active and it goes to that reply. A thread can have up to 24 replies, and each one follows its network's media rules.
