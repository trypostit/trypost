---
title: Post thumbnails in Insights that don't expire
date: 2026-10-13
tags: [fixed]
---

Instagram and TikTok sign their preview links and expire them after a few days, so thumbnails in Insights used to break.

Top posts and the channel post table now show the image or video you published with TryPost, served from our own CDN. The network's preview is used only for posts made outside TryPost.

Post type tags are clearer too: you'll see **Reels**, **Story** and **Video**, with the same icons as your publish list.
