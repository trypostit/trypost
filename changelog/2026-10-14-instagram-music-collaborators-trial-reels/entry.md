---
title: Music, collaborators and trial reels for Instagram
date: 2026-10-14
tag: new
---

Your Instagram reels and feed posts can now carry everything Instagram's publishing API offers.

- **Music:** search Instagram's audio library, preview a track and set the music and video volume. Music is available on accounts connected through Facebook. If yours uses Instagram Login, **Save & link** reconnects the same channel through Facebook and keeps its posts.
- **Collaborators:** invite up to three Instagram accounts as co-authors on a feed post, carousel or reel.
- **Trial reels:** share a reel with non-followers first, then let Instagram graduate it automatically or do it yourself.
