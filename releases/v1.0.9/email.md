# Changelog v1.0.9 — Introducing webhooks and more...

By TryPost Product Team • [Release v1.0.9](https://github.com/trypostit/trypost/releases/tag/v1.0.9)

Hello! Welcome to this week's update. Here's what's new in TryPost.

## Introducing webhooks

There's a Webhooks page in the workspace sidebar. You give us a URL, pick the post events you care about, and we POST to that URL when they happen. Created, scheduled, published, failed, deleted: whatever you subscribed to.

You can send a test event, look at each delivery, and replay one if it missed.
