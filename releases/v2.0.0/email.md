# Changelog v2.0.0 — Every channel gets its own post, workspace analytics and more...

By TryPost Product Team • [Release v2.0.0](https://github.com/trypostit/trypost/releases/tag/v2.0.0)

Hello! Welcome to this week's update. This one is big enough that we're calling it TryPost 2.0. Here's what's new.

## Each channel gets its own post

Until now, a post sent to five networks was really one post with five copies glued together. Move the Instagram time and you moved it everywhere. Now every channel gets its own post, with its own text, media, settings, schedule and notes. You still write once in the composer, and "Customize for each network" lets you adjust each one before saving. After that, you can reschedule, edit or delete the LinkedIn version and the others stay as they are.

## See how your whole workspace is doing

Insights now shows the whole workspace at once. TryPost records your follower counts every day, pulls in your recent posts from each network and keeps the numbers, so you can put this month next to last month. If you use the TryPost API or an AI assistant connected to TryPost, the same reports are available there.

## Edit images without leaving the composer

Click an image and an editor opens with crop presets for each network, alt text, filters, and sliders for brightness, contrast, saturation and warmth. If one image in an Instagram carousel has a ratio Instagram won't accept, the composer points at that slide and offers to crop it for you.

## New features

- LinkedIn and Facebook text posts with a link now show the link preview card
- YouTube Shorts can have a description separate from the post text
- Instagram feed images can keep their original ratio
- On phones, the calendar shows each post as a small network icon instead of a cramped label
- Notification emails got a redesign, with a preview of the post and a button to open it on the network

## Fixes

- Typing `<` (like "I <3 TryPost") no longer cuts off the rest of your post
- TikTok photo posts accept images added by URL or upload
- X posts with a video still processing wait and retry instead of failing
- Instagram posts whose media isn't ready yet retry instead of failing
- Posts you published through TryPost no longer come back as duplicate imports
- TikTok posts no longer show another video's link and stats
- Bluesky asks you to confirm your email before connecting, so video uploads don't fail later
- The image editor's controls no longer overlap the image on phones

Paulo from TryPost.it
