# Changelog v2.0.1 — Drag posts to a new time, music on Instagram reels and more...

By TryPost Product Team • [Release v2.0.1](https://github.com/trypostit/trypost/releases/tag/v2.0.1)

Hello! Welcome to this week's update. Here's what's new in TryPost.

## Drag a post on the calendar to change its time

Moving a post used to mean opening it, changing the date and saving. Now you grab it on the calendar and drop it on another hour or day, in the day, week or month view. Drop a scheduled post on a free posting time of its channel and it joins the queue there. Drag a draft out of the No Date panel to give it a date, or drop any post on that panel to take it off the schedule. It works with a mouse and on a phone, and you'll see a message confirming each move.

## Music, collaborators and trial reels on Instagram

Your reels can now have music from Instagram's library. There's a catch: the account has to be connected through Facebook, because that's the only way Instagram lets apps use its music. If you connected with Instagram Login, you can link the channel through Facebook and it keeps its posts. You can also invite up to three collaborators on a feed post, carousel or reel, and publish a reel as a trial reel, which Instagram shows to people who don't follow you first.

## A clearer Insights, ready to share

Insights got a cleaner look, with one card per section and charts in colors that stay readable if you're color blind. In the Top 5, the metric you sort by now comes first, and the Instagram thumbnails that sometimes showed up broken load again. The Followers chart, the channel Metrics card and the Summary also have a new share button. It turns the numbers into an image you can download, copy, or attach to a new post straight away.

## New features

- What's New: a card in the sidebar tells you about the latest update, and the Help menu lists all of them
- If you use TryPost inside ChatGPT, it now offers to sign in right in the conversation
- New accounts start in your browser's language instead of English
- The composer asks before you lose unsaved changes, remembers the channels you used last, and its search ignores accents
- Sent posts have Copy link and Share link, and notes open inside the post card
- The media menu shows the real logos of Canva, Google Drive, Google Photos and Unsplash

## Fixes

- LinkedIn posts with parentheses, brackets or symbols no longer get cut off after the first sentence
- Mastodon posts no longer show HTML tags like `<p>` in the text
- TikTok posts that went live are no longer marked as failed
- Bluesky channels no longer lose their connection when TryPost refreshes the login
- TikTok photo carousels and YouTube Shorts show the right post type in Insights
- Linking an older Instagram channel through Facebook no longer fails with "Page not found"
- A reel's music shows its name and cover even when it was added through the API or an AI assistant

Cheers,
Paulo from TryPost.it
