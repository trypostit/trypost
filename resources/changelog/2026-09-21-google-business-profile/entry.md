---
title: Google Business Profile is now on TryPost
date: 2026-09-21
tags: [new]
---

Post to your Google Business Profile from the same place you plan the rest of your week. Connect each business location as its own channel and schedule for it like any other network.

- **What's New:** share news and updates, with a photo and a button like Book, Order online or Call now.
- **Events:** add a title and dates so people see what's coming up.
- **Offers:** promote a sale with a coupon code, a link to redeem it and your terms.
