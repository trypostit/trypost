This update is big enough that we're calling it TryPost 2.0. Here's what changed.

- **Each channel gets its own post:** every channel keeps its own text, media, settings and schedule. Write once, use **Customize for each network** to adjust, then edit or reschedule the LinkedIn version without touching the others.
- **Workspace insights:** see every channel together, with follower counts recorded daily and this month next to last month.
- **Image editor:** crop for each network, add alt text, and adjust filters, brightness, contrast, saturation and warmth without leaving the composer.
