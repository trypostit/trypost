---
title: Drag posts on the calendar to reschedule them
date: 2026-10-29
tags: [new]
---

Moving a post to another day or time now takes one drag on the calendar.

- **Every calendar view:** drop a post on an hour to move it there, or on a day to keep its time. Drafts stay drafts, and a recurring series follows the post you move.
- **Posting times:** drop a scheduled post on one of its channel's posting times and it joins the queue there. Anywhere else, it gets a custom time, and you confirm first if it was in the queue.
- **No Date:** drag a post in to take it off the schedule and keep it as a draft, or drag a draft out onto the calendar to give it a date.
- **Status at a glance:** every post on the calendar shows an icon for its status, with its name when you hover it.
- **On your phone:** press and hold a post, then drag it to another day or time.
