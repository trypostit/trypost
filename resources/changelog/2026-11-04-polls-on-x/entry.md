---
title: Polls for X are here 🎉
date: 2026-11-04
tags: [new]
---

You can now create X polls right from TryPost and find out what your followers think.

- **On a post or in a thread:** ask a question, give a few choices and schedule the poll like any other post, on its own or as part of a thread.
- **Results that come to you:** each poll in Sent shows how the votes are splitting and when voting ends.
- **Synced from X:** polls you post directly on X show up here automatically, results included, so every poll you run lives in one place.
