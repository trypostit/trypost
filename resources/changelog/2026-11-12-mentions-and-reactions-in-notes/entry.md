---
title: Better notes for collaborating on posts
date: 2026-11-12
tags: [improved]
---

Talking a post through with your team is now easier, right where the post lives.

- **Mentions:** type @ in a note to bring a teammate in, or @all for everyone.
- **Reactions:** answer a note with any emoji.
- **Live:** new notes and reactions show up for everyone the moment they're added.
- **Get notified:** you get an email when someone mentions you or adds a note to a post you're part of.
- **Works anywhere:** web, MCP and API.
