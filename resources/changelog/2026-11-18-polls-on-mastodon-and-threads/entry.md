---
title: Polls are here for Mastodon and Threads 🎉
date: 2026-11-18
tags: [new]
---

Got a question for your followers? Now you can ask it with a poll on Mastodon and Threads, right from TryPost, just like on X.

- **On Mastodon:** add up to four choices and keep the poll open from 5 minutes to 30 days. Let people pick more than one answer, hide the votes until it ends, or turn any reply in a thread into a poll.
- **On Threads:** add up to four choices to a text post, and Threads takes care of how long it stays open.
- **See how it's going:** every poll in Sent shows how the votes are splitting and when voting ends.
- **All your polls in one place:** polls you post directly on Mastodon or Threads show up here too, results included.
