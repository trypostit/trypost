---
title: Polls for Mastodon and Threads are here 🎉
date: 2026-11-18
tags: [new]
---

You can now run polls on Mastodon and Threads from TryPost, the same way you do on X.

- **Mastodon:** give up to four choices and pick how long voting stays open, from 5 minutes to 30 days. Turn on **Allow multiple choices** or **Hide votes until it ends** when you need them, and make any reply in a thread a poll too.
- **Threads:** add up to four choices to a text post. Threads decides how long voting stays open.
- **Results that come to you:** each poll in Sent shows how the votes are splitting and when voting ends.
- **Synced from the networks:** polls you post directly on Mastodon or Threads show up here automatically, results included.
