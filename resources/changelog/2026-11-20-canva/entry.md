---
title: Design your posts in Canva, right from TryPost
date: 2026-11-20
tags: [new]
---

You can now design a post's image in Canva without leaving TryPost or downloading the file first.

- **Start a design:** in the composer, click the add media button, choose **Canva** and pick a size: square, portrait, landscape or story.
- **Back in your post:** finish the design in Canva and it shows up in the post you were writing.
- **Edit in Canva:** to change a design later, open it from its thumbnail with **Edit in Canva**. The new version replaces the old one.
